OUR MANIFESTO

Knowledge does not end.

Neither should learning.

A useful question rarely fits inside one lesson. You start with a paper, get stuck on an equation, open the code, and discover something you need to understand first.

Learning should let you follow that path without losing where you started.

We’re building Rabbit Hole so you can explore sources, ask questions and try ideas on one connected canvas.

The next step should depend on you.

If a concept is unfamiliar, work through the foundations. If you already understand it, go deeper.

AI gives us a chance to build learning that responds to your questions and understanding, instead of sending everyone through the same sequence.

Understanding comes from using an idea.

Read the explanation. Then change a parameter, work through a problem, or run the code. See whether you can explain what happened.

Getting stuck tells you what to work on next. Finishing a lesson alone doesn’t tell you what you understood.

Keep the thread.

Your sources, questions, experiments and conclusions belong together.

When you return tomorrow, you should be able to continue from what you already learned. When a question takes you somewhere unexpected, you should be able to explore and find your way back.

That is the kind of learning we want to make possible.
