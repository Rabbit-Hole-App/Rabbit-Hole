Combine the graphic confidence of oversized black grotesk typography, generous white space, black-and-white engraved/halftone science illustrations, tiny precise utility labels, and a single muted candy-pink learning path. Reference the visual qualities of typesafe.ai: bold white/pink fields, strong typographic scale, retro utility-window details, crisp pixel/halftone imagery. Make the composition original and appropriate to adaptive learning. It must feel like a carefully art-directed real website, not a generic AI SaaS template.

COLOR AND TYPE:

Small annotations use an understated monospace. Illustration texture is deliberate monochrome halftone; do not apply noisy texture over text. No gradients, blue, purple, neon, glassmorphism, glossy 3D blobs, soft drop-shadow cards, or rainbow colors.
