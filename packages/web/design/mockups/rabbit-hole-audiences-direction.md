# Who is Rabbit Hole for?

Status: rejected visual proposal, retained as history. The user rejected the
generated art and displaying all six audiences simultaneously. The approved
replacement is documented in `docs/features/rabbit-hole-audiences.md`: one
workflow preview at a time, scroll chapters on desktop and swipe/manual selection
on mobile. Do not use this image or six-column layout in the landing page.

## Intent and placement

Help visitors recognize a reason to use an adaptive learning platform within a
few seconds. Place the section in the existing white space between the black
manifesto passage and the pink clouds. Preserve the rest of the landing page.
Keep the existing geometric display type, black ink, white page and pink accent.

Use six clear audience names, each paired with a learning outcome. Writers for
Medium, Towards Data Science and Substack belong with creators; naming those
platforms describes the audience, not an integration or partnership.

| Audience | Main outcome / draft copy | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creators & writers | Turn deep research into something worth sharing. | How do I explain attention clearly? |
| AI / ML engineers | Understand the paper. Connect it to the code. | How does this model work in practice? |
| Educators | Make difficult ideas click. | How can I teach this through an example? |
| University students | Go beyond memorizing. | Why does this result follow? |
| Enterprise onboarding | Find your way through the code. | Where does this request go next? |
| Interview preparation | Explain your thinking. | Can I defend this model choice? |

This is draft positioning, not a promise of publishing integrations, an LMS,
automated interview scoring or production enterprise provisioning.

## Three directions considered

1. **Expanding illustrated chapters (recommended).** Six adjacent editorial
   columns, one open into a small research/learning workspace. All audiences stay
   visible and their objects express different uses. Most direct to understand;
   needs a separate vertical treatment on phones.
2. **A shared research desk.** Audience-labelled papers, code maps and study
   notes arranged across one large work surface. More atmospheric and unusual;
   the overlap makes comparing audiences harder and small screens more complex.
3. **Questions leading into a hole.** Six sample questions connected to one
   opening, revealing their audience when selected. Strong Rabbit Hole metaphor;
   weaker immediate audience recognition and repeats the existing portal motif.

## Recommended visual

Eyebrow: Who is it for?

Headline: Different minds. Deeper questions.

Introduction: For the things you want to understand, and the ideas you want to share.

Six horizontal chapters sit between thin rules, with one expanded chapter.
Its quiet blush field contains an engraved/dithered arrangement of sources,
annotations and an example question. The other chapters keep their audience
names, short outcomes and smaller ink drawings visible. No rotating labels,
floating card shadows, invented metrics or testimonial logos.

The imagery follows the job: manuscript and citations, paper/code study,
teaching diagram, student notes, repository map, interview explanation.
It is not a literal illustration of six stereotyped people.

## Proposed interaction, not yet implemented

- Click/tap or keyboard selection expands a chapter; pointer hover previews a
  subtle ink/accent response rather than constantly replacing selected content.
- The selected chapter opens over roughly 350–450 ms. Source fragments shift
  into an annotated learning workspace. Nothing spins continuously.
- Scroll reveals the section once without capturing or slowing page scrolling.
- Every audience remains readable without hovering. On mobile, use six stacked
  accordion headings with one detail area open, without horizontal page scrolling.
- Reduced motion changes selection immediately. Drawings are decorative; all
  positioning and meaningful example text remain real accessible HTML later.
- The concept's “Explore your use case” is a proposed detail interaction. Do not
  ship that link without a useful destination or working expansion behavior.
