# Rabbit Hole docs — Field Guide concept

Visual mockup only. No docs route or interactive search is implemented yet.
Created with the built-in image generation tool, not the paid API fallback.

Reference inspected: https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction — useful navigation,
search, article and contents structure. The visual direction below is original
to Rabbit Hole and preserves the existing site's typography and ring identity.

## Prompt

Create one high-fidelity desktop website UI mockup, landscape 1536 x 1024, of
Rabbit Hole documentation. Show a single full-width browser content viewport;
no device frame, perspective, multiple screens or presentation-board labels.
This is a docs product, not a promotional landing-page hero. The concept is
"Rabbit Hole Field Guide": an editorial handbook for an adaptive learning app.

Use warm ivory #faf9f6, near-black #101010, hairline warm-gray separators, a
restrained dusty-pink selected navigation row, and tiny brick-red route markers.
Space Grotesk-like geometric headings and Inter-like very legible body type.
No gradients, glow, giant pill cards, fake analytics, mascots or photography.
The Rabbit Hole logo is six nested tilted oval rings receding into one opening.

Layout: 76px top bar; left index about 245px wide; main article about 760px;
right contents rail about 190px with generous gutters. Thin vertical rules
separate columns. Every column aligns to a deliberate grid.

Header left: small ring logo and "Rabbit Hole", with a quiet "Field Guide"
label. Center a real-looking search control "Search the guide..." with "Ctrl K"
key hint. Right: "Back to site" and a compact black "Open Rabbit Hole ↗" button.

Left index: small "GET STARTED" label, selected "Introduction" with a tiny
brick-red dot and soft pink row; "Your first rabbit hole"; "Bring your sources".
Then "LEARN" with "Ask better questions", "Interactive canvases", "Practice &
review", "Notes & citations". Then "YOUR WORKSPACE" with "Projects & history",
"Sharing & exports", "Usage & credits". Use restrained line icons and obvious
typographic grouping. Footer of index: "Need a hand?" and "Email us ↗".

Main article top: breadcrumb "Get started / Introduction" and an understated
"Copy page" action. Small monospace label "FIELD NOTE 01". Heading, about 56px:
"Start with a question." Supporting paragraph, readable 18px: "Rabbit Hole
turns your sources and questions into learning you can explore, practice, and
come back to." Under it, a modest horizontal editorial figure, around 190px
tall: an engraved black wireframe opening with a few small paper/source shapes
connected by a thin winding route. Give the illustration plenty of ivory space;
it should be a diagram in a handbook, not a large decorative hero. Caption:
"A source. A question. A path of your own."

Below the diagram: heading "Your first rabbit hole" and the sentence "Bring
something you want to understand. We’ll help you find a way in." Then three
compact readable steps linked by a thin brick-red vertical margin line and
small outlined number circles: "01 Bring a source" / "Start with a repository,
paper, document, or question."; "02 Follow the question" / "Explore an
explanation, ask why, and open an interactive canvas."; "03 Make it stick" /
"Save your notes, practice what you learn, and return when you need it."
At the lower edge include a quiet outlined next-chapter link "Next: Your first
rabbit hole →", not a marketing card grid.

Right rail: "ON THIS PAGE", a vertical trail with "The idea", active "Your
first rabbit hole", "Where to go next"; below a small callout headed "Go one
level deeper" with "How adaptive learning works ↗". A tiny ring motif can sit
at the foot of this rail. Keep this decorative mark subordinate to navigation.

Creative signature: a calm printed field-guide aesthetic, one diagram, chapter
index, brick-red marginal route, and generous whitespace. Usability is primary.
Render precise crisp typography, realistic spacing and polished actual web UI.
