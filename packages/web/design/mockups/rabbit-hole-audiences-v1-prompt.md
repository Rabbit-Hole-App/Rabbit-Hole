# Audience section — built-in image generation prompt

Built-in image generation; new visual mockup, no API-key fallback.

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished, high-fidelity desktop web design mockup of the new "Who is it for?" section for Rabbit Hole, an adaptive learning platform. This is an actual website section design for visual review, not a poster or marketing illustration. Wide landscape image about 1800 x 1200. Show the whole section. No device frame, no browser chrome, no repeated navigation. Include a thin black band at the very top indicating the previous black section, a white content area, and only a sliver of blush pink clouds at the very bottom indicating the next existing section.

Design concept: SIX EXPANDING EDITORIAL CHAPTERS, one expanded. A spacious horizontal interactive accordion, six audience chapters with thin black vertical dividing rules. They are one continuous editorial composition, not six floating cards. The first chapter is expanded and occupies 34% of the width; the other five share the remaining width. All six audience names remain legible horizontally in compact wrapped headings, never rotated. Desktop content margins about 85px. Generous whitespace. Asymmetric active composition. Restrained monochrome engraved / dither ink drawings, clean black Space Grotesk-like typography and Inter-like body copy, tiny monospaced indexing. White (#ffffff), ink (#101010), pale muted pink (#f1dce4) only. No blue, green, purple, gradients, shiny 3D, stairs, tree roots, mascot, people, stock photos, planetary diagrams, or giant circular portal.

Top layout: small uppercase eyebrow "WHO IS IT FOR", followed by a large black editorial heading left aligned, exactly:
"Different minds.
Deeper questions."
At its right a concise small introduction:
"For the things you want to understand,
and the ideas you want to share."

Below, place the accordion across nearly the whole width. Height about 580 px. One horizontal hairline rule above and below all six columns.
Chapter 01 is expanded, a very pale dusty-pink background with no corner rounding. Its top has "01", heading "Creators & writers", and readable copy "Turn deep research into something worth sharing." Under it a small tag line "Essays / newsletters / video". Its large original ink vignette is a modern research work surface: three overlapping annotated article sheets, a simple pen, margin citation marks and one small branching idea diagram connecting reference snippets to a draft essay. All form a composed 2D black-and-white printed technical collage, not a photograph. At the bottom show a concise real-use question in a small outlined text field: "How do I explain attention clearly?" and a simple underlined text link "Explore your use case" with a tiny arrow. Keep the active scene anchored to the article task, not a generic laptop picture.

The other five chapters are narrow, white, with a small number, a clear dark heading and a short payoff. Beneath each is one small distinctive vertical monochrome ink vignette, each visually independent and naturally spaced:
02 "AI / ML engineers" — "Understand it. Build on it." Visual: code listing crossed with a compact technical neural-network diagram.
03 "Educators" — "Make difficult ideas click." Visual: an open lesson book and a simple chalk-style geometry construction.
04 "University students" — "Go beyond memorizing." Visual: annotated study notes, a pencil and a modest equation.
05 "Enterprise onboarding" — "Find your way through the code." Visual: a repository map with a few connected code-file sheets.
06 "Interview preparation" — "Explain your thinking." Visual: two question/answer sheets and a sketched model-performance graph.
These are beautiful fine engraving and subtle halftone illustrations rather than UI icon badges. No labels or graphics may collide with neighboring columns. Make the narrow headings around 18px, active heading 28px, main headline around 75px. Body copy must be readable. Use only the specified text, no fake metrics, testimonials, partner logos, pricing, or extra CTAs.

At lower right beneath the section's bottom rule, small understated text "Six ways in. Your own way through." This mockup shows one selected state; do not pretend the image itself is animated. Overall should feel like a sophisticated curiosity field guide with a highly original expanding editorial interface, harmonious with a white/black/pink Rabbit Hole landing page.
