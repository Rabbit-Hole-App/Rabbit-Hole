# Blog hero scenes

The saved settings for blog heroes (owner, 2026-11-13: the background must not be the same in every image). Each post gets a
different one: `scripts/landing-art.mjs blog-hero|blog-post` takes `--scene <name>` or, without it, the first scene below that no
earlier hero has used (a hero's prompt record, `design/art/<slug>-hero.prompt.md`, names its scene; `garden` belongs to the first
post). Add a scene by adding a line `name: description` under "Scenes". The subject sentences place the characters in the scene,
including where the Cheshire Cat sits, so the tree and bough are only used when the scene has one.

## Scenes

garden: an overgrown garden of tall rose hedges and twisting old trees, a mad tea-party table set with teapots and cups, playing-card soldiers standing guard, giant mushrooms and oversized flowers, a grandfather clock among the trees, a tiny locked door at the foot of a great tree, hanging oil lamps, playing cards drifting through the air, a distant castle with a checkerboard floor, and the dark mouth of a rabbit hole between tree roots near the right edge
library: an enormous hollow-tree library and study, towering curved bookshelves growing out of the roots and walls and rising out of sight, rolling ladders, stacks of leaning books, a long reading table with an open atlas and candles, a brass globe and an armillary sphere, hanging oil lamps, a grandfather clock, scrolls and maps pinned up, and a big round window showing rolling hills and a distant castle
riverbank: a wide slow river with reeds and water lilies, a small wooden rowing boat tied to a mossy bank, willow trees trailing into the water, stepping stones, a humped stone bridge, dragonflies, a heron standing in the shallows, misty blue hills and a far-off castle, a few playing cards floating on the water
croquet-lawn: a formal croquet lawn edged with clipped topiary hedges and a hedge maze, playing-card soldiers bent double as hoops, flamingo mallets and curled-up hedgehog balls on the grass, a striped marquee, a stone balustrade with urns, tall poplars, and the Queen's palace with checkerboard terraces in the distance
mushroom-forest: a misty forest of giant mushrooms towering like trees with gills and spotted caps, ferns as tall as houses, a fairy ring, dew-covered spider webs, snails, tiny arched footbridges between the caps, glow-worm lanterns, drifting spores and a thin stream, with far-off pale blue hills
courtroom: the King and Queen's courtroom, a tall judge's bench with a heavy curtain and a gavel, a jury box of small animals, tiers of benches full of playing-card courtiers, tall arched windows, tarts on a platter and scrolls on a table, hanging chandeliers, and a checkerboard floor
chess-fields: a wide countryside divided by hedges and brooks into a huge chessboard of green and cream squares, giant carved chess pieces standing in the fields, small stone bridges over the brooks, signposts pointing in every direction, windmills and distant hills, and a few playing cards blowing across the sky
