# Blog hero palettes

The saved colour schemes for blog heroes (owner, 2026-11-13: the colour must differ from image to image as well as the
background). Every scheme stays inside the hero palette of `BLOG_IMAGE_GUIDE.md` (paper, plum ink, plum, sage, powder blue, ochre,
at most one rose accent) and changes which colour dominates the scenery. `scripts/landing-art.mjs blog-hero|blog-post` takes
`--palette <name>` or, without it, the first palette below that no earlier hero has used (a hero's prompt record names its
palette; `rose` belongs to the first post). The characters keep the colours of `characters.md` in every scheme. Add one by adding
a line `name: description` under "Palettes".

## Palettes

blue: powder blue (#A9BCCB) and slate blue-grey dominate the scenery, the sky, water, stone and shadows, with cream highlights and pale sage in the plants and a little warm ochre on wood
ochre: warm ochre (#D8C08C), honey and cream dominate the scenery, the wood, paper, sand and light, with pale sage in the plants and powder blue only in small patches of sky
lavender: pale lavender and plum-grey (#8A7AA6) dominate the scenery, the stone, shadows and flowers, with cream highlights and pale sage in the plants and a little ochre on wood
teal: dusty teal and sea-green dominate the scenery, the foliage, water and distant hills, with cream highlights, pale sage and a little ochre on wood
sage: sage and moss green (#B7C2B0) dominate the scenery, the foliage and grass, with cream highlights, warm ochre on wood and powder blue only in the sky
