# Blog hero characters

The saved character sheet for every blog hero (owner, 2026-11-13: the characters must stay the same from image to image).
`scripts/landing-art.mjs blog-hero|blog-post` pastes the lines under "Sheet" into every prompt, word for word, so edit them
here and nowhere else. They follow the first hero (`rabbit-holes-not-courses`). Subject sentences name a character and say what
it does; they never re-describe its look or clothes. Dusty rose appears only on the White Rabbit's jacket (the palette allows
one rose accent per image), so the scenery takes its colour from sage, powder blue, ochre, lavender and plum.

## Sheet

White Rabbit: a white rabbit standing upright like a small gentleman, long upright ears, a short dusty-rose jacket with lapels over a pale waistcoat, a gold pocket watch on a chain, small bright eye, drawn in Tenniel's engraved manner.
Alice: a girl with long wavy light-brown hair worn loose to her waist and no headband, a pale lavender dress with short puffed sleeves, a white pinafore apron, white stockings and black Mary Jane shoes.
Mad Hatter: a thin gaunt man with a long face and a large nose, a very tall brown top hat with a paper tag tucked in its band, a large bow tie, a long muted-ochre tailcoat and checked trousers, a teacup and saucer in his hand.
Caterpillar: a plump blue-grey caterpillar sitting upright on a toadstool, drawn small, smoking a long hookah, with a calm heavy-lidded face.
Cheshire Cat: a large grey-plum striped tabby with a very wide fully drawn grin and bright eyes, stretched along a tree bough.
