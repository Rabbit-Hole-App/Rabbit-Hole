# Blog hero characters

The saved character sheet for every blog hero (owner, 2026-11-13: the characters must stay the same from image to image).
`scripts/landing-art.mjs blog-hero|blog-post` pastes the lines under "Sheet" into every prompt, word for word, so edit them
here and nowhere else. They follow the first hero (`rabbit-holes-not-courses`), which is also kept as the reference image `characters-reference.jpg` that the pipeline hands to the model with every prompt. Subject sentences name a character and say what
it does; they never re-describe its look or clothes. Dusty rose appears only on the White Rabbit's jacket (the palette allows
one rose accent per image), so the scenery takes its colour from sage, powder blue, ochre, lavender and plum.

## Sheet

White Rabbit: a fluffy white rabbit with finely hatched fur, tall upright ears and a small dark eye, dressed like a gentleman in a muted dusty-rose crimson frock jacket with a high collar and lapels over a cream waistcoat and a small dark cravat, a gold pocket watch on a chain held in one paw, drawn like Tenniel's rabbit.
Alice: a slim girl with long wavy golden-blonde hair worn loose past her shoulders and no headband, a pale lavender dress with short puffed sleeves and a ruffled hem, a white pinafore apron with a frilled edge and a bow at the back, white stockings and flat black Mary Jane shoes.
Mad Hatter: a thin man with a long nose and a long gaunt face seen in profile, a mop of dark curly hair, a very tall dark grey top hat with a paper tag tucked in its band, a large patterned bow tie, a long muted-mustard ochre frock coat, checked knee breeches with dark stockings and black shoes, a teacup and saucer held in one hand.
Caterpillar: a blue-green segmented caterpillar with a ringed striped body and a small calm face, sitting upright on whatever raised perch the scene gives it (a mushroom cap, a toadstool or a stack of books), drawn small, smoking a tall brass hookah.
Cheshire Cat: a large grey-plum tabby with dark stripes, big round eyes and a very wide fully drawn toothy grin, lounging lazily on whatever perch the scene gives it.
