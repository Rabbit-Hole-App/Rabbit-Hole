inward toward the text. Socrates introduces questioning; Feynman accompanies
active understanding. They have no room, photo panel, card, border or backdrop.

and a soft lower alpha mask integrate the figures into the ivory/stone page.
The landing page's approved standing conversation remains unchanged.
The rejected blackboard image is retained as an unused historical asset.
