scores = [2, 1, 0, −1]

shifted = scores - scores.max()
weights = shifted.exp()
probs = weights / weights.sum()

01What is Rabbit Hole?

Rabbit Hole is an adaptive learning platform for following questions, understanding ideas, and going deeper at your own pace.

02What does adaptive learning mean?

It means the path responds to the learner. Start with what you already understand, spend time on what is unclear, and choose the next step around your goals.

03Do I need to know where to start?

A question is enough. Pick something you want to understand and begin there. You do not need to map the entire subject before taking the first step.

04How deep can I go?

Follow the idea as far as it is useful to you. Get an overview, explore an example, or unpack the details behind a concept. The depth follows your curiosity.

05How is it different from a chatbot?

The focus is the learning journey: connecting questions, explanations, and practice into understanding you can build on. An answer is a starting point for the next question.

06Where do I begin?

Bring something you are curious about. Get started, or take a look at the features first.
