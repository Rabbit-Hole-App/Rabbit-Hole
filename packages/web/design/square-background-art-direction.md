opacity and edge masks. Both images are 1254 × 1254 despite the requested
1024 × 1024 canvas. The depth image is RGB; the paths image includes alpha.

## Depth prompt

Create one original square 1024 x 1024 decorative image asset for the white background of Rabbit Hole, an adaptive learning website. Use case: stylized-concept. Subject: eight to twelve nested square architectural wireframes receding into a small off-centre opening, like an impossible spatial diagram on an old computer printout. A few delicate dotted paths lead between the frames. Compose one coherent airy square-shaped diagram, subtly oblique with visible depth, with much open negative space around and within it. Fine graphite-grey etched lines, uneven photocopy/halftone stipple, occasional tiny square pixels, and just two small very muted dusty-pink accents. The whole illustration is quiet pale grey with a slightly darker tiny inner opening; no large solid black masses. Vintage technical print / retro scientific diagram aesthetic, not a glossy 3D render. Outer lines gently fragment and fade into white toward all four edges so the asset blends seamlessly into a white webpage. Background pure white, with no paper tint, no texture in empty background areas, no border framing the whole picture, no shadow, no page mockup, no interface, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no characters, no stairs. This is decorative abstract artwork, not a literal screenshot or product demonstration. Preserve plenty of white, delicate geometry, and readable square structures. Generate the image itself only.

## Paths prompt

Create one original square 1024 x 1024 decorative image asset for the white background of Rabbit Hole, an adaptive learning website. Use case: stylized-concept. Subject: an airy abstract network of twelve to eighteen square wireframe apertures of varied sizes, linked by delicate thin straight and dotted routes. Some routes fork, some converge, and two or three squares contain smaller nested squares suggesting different paths into learning. A sparse, thoughtfully composed geometric constellation, with a gentle sense of architectural perspective and open space between its parts. Distribute the diagram asymmetrically across the middle of the square with smaller fragments thinning out toward the edges. Fine graphite-grey etched lines, uneven photocopy/halftone stipple, occasional tiny square pixels, and exactly two or three very muted dusty-pink square accents. Mostly pale grey; no large solid black masses. Vintage technical print / retro scientific diagram aesthetic, not a glossy 3D render. Outer lines gently fragment and fade into white toward all four edges so the asset blends seamlessly into a white webpage. Background pure white, with no paper tint, no texture in empty background areas, no border framing the whole picture, no shadow, no page mockup, no interface cards, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no characters, no plants or tree, no stairs, no central tunnel. This is decorative abstract artwork, not a literal product diagram. Keep the squares legible, the pathways graceful, and the negative space generous. Generate the image itself only.
