# Landing logo sources

Official files only, retrieved 2026-10-28. Never redrawn or recoloured. An organisation without an official file is NOT published (`file: null` in `src/landing/logos-data.js`); the strip shows only entries that have a file.

| Organisation | File | Source | Retrieved | Usage terms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google | `google.svg` | Google-hosted official wordmark: https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googlelogo/svg/googlelogo_clr_74x24px.svg (the file Google serves from its own branding path; Brand Resource Center: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/) | 2026-10-28 | https://about.google/brand-resource-center/brand-elements/ . Owner to confirm this reference-use is acceptable. |
| Shopify | `shopify.svg` | Shopify brand assets zip `shopify-primary-logo.zip`, `01 - Logo/svg/shopify_logo_whitebg.svg`: https://cdn.shopify.com/static/brand-assets/shopify-primary-logo.zip (linked from https://www.shopify.com/brand-assets) | 2026-10-28 | https://www.shopify.com/brand-assets |
| Facebook | `facebook.png` (official pack ships PNG and .ai only, no SVG) | Meta Facebook Brand Asset Pack, `Logo/Primary Logo/Facebook_Logo_Primary.png`, from https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/ | 2026-10-28 | https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/guidelines/ |

## Not published (NEEDS-OFFICIAL-SOURCE)

| Organisation | Why |
|---|---|

| Stanford University | Logo files on https://identity.stanford.edu/visual-identity/stanford-logos/ are not downloadable without Stanford sign-in/request. |
| Harvard University | https://brand.harvard.edu/ was unreachable from this machine. |
