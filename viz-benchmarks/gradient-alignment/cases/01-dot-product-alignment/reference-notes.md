# Reference notes — dot-product alignment

## Learning objective
A learner should be able to read the sign and rough magnitude of a dot
product between two vectors as an alignment signal, the way it is used when
comparing gradient directions during training.

## Teaching pattern
Two labelled vectors, drawn as the values themselves rather than an abstract
diagram, feeding a single worked equation.

## Important visual relationships
The equation's operands must visibly correspond to the two vectors drawn
beside it.

## What to match
- teaching clarity
- concept coverage
- hierarchy
- spatial relationships
- progressive disclosure
