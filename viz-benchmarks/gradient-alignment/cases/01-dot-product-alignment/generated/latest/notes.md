# Iteration v001 - static

## What this is

Two labelled 2D vectors (`g1`, `g2`), drawn as strips with their raw values,
each annotated with a short caption, feeding a single worked equation for
their dot product. The point is that a reader can look at the two vectors
and the worked number together and read alignment directly, rather than
being told the answer in prose.

## Patterns exercised

`flow` (vectors into a single worked result) and `matrix_operation` (the
dot product itself) pass. No PRIMITIVE or TEMPLATE gap blocked anything.

## Did this case require a bespoke scene workaround?

No.
