# /explain gpt-oss-120b benchmark log — 2026-11-08

Owner decisions for this benchmark (2026-11-08): the written `/explain` author moves off Opus to `gpt-oss-120b`; Fireworks first, Cerebras second, Gemini `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` last as the production fallback. The benchmark measures the whole card (text, SVG diagrams, Recraft pictures) end to end at reasoning effort low, medium and high, with the fallback chain OFF (a fallback is a failure, not normal behaviour). ### Method

- **Local stack:** the server ran on the owner's machine (`C:/s/spec/latency-lab/explainbench/stack.sh`, wrangler dev, local D1/R2). The provider calls were real: Fireworks `accounts/fireworks/models/gpt-oss-120b` and Recraft V4.1 Flash. Settings in `.dev.vars` (`addkey-fast.cjs`): `EXPLANATION_MODEL_PROFILE=fireworks-<effort>`, `EXPLANATION_FALLBACK=off`, `EXPLANATION_INTERACTIONS=true`, `IMAGE_GENERATE_ENABLED=true`, plus the image hold and maximum-charge maps copied verbatim from `packages/web/wrangler.rabbit-hole-prod.jsonc`. `SMALL_ENV=dev` replaced the stack's `test`, so spend is enforced and every call is admitted and settled on the ledger; `USAGE_PLATFORM_DAY_USD=10` is the dev worker's value. No Anthropic key was on the stack, so no Opus call was possible. The stack was restarted for each effort with a fresh persist directory.
- **Harness** `C:/s/spec/latency-lab/explainbench/fastbench.mjs`: one test user per effort and one new canvas per card. It sends `POST /api/learn/artifact {app, command:'explanation', args, board:'main'}`, Standard Depth, timed from request to response. The server answers only after the pictures are made. Cards ran one at a time, 1.5 s apart: run 1 of all 20 prompts with the cases interleaved, then run 2. The first call after a restart is cold. Cost per card is the user's `/api/learn/usage` usedCredits before and after the card. The server log (`app.log`) gives the author call times, repair causes and picture outcomes. Prompts are in `fastprompts.mjs`.
- **Browser cards:** run 2 of the first prompt in each case, 5 per effort, sent through the canvas composer's Send (headless Chromium, `/api/learn/ask` blocked). Timed from the Send click until the text, every SVG and every picture were shown, then again after a page reload.
- Aggregation: `fastaggregate.mjs`. The blind-review set holds the 39 delivered cards: shuffled, no effort or model label, and the id-to-effort map kept separately.

"within 4 s" counts delivered cards at or under 4.0 s out of all cards. "author call p50" is the sum of a delivered card's author calls (the first call plus its repair). "picture stage" is total time minus author time, for cards with pictures.

**Where the time goes (author calls that completed):** low p50 3.1 s, p90 4.6 s, min 1.7 s (77 calls). Medium p50 6.2 s, p90 7.5 s (33 calls; 19 more cut at 8 s). High: only 1 of 40 first calls finished inside 8 s (7.9 s). The picture stage adds a median of about 2.2 s. It starts only after the author's card passes validation, and pictures run in parallel with each other. **Repairs:** 37 of 40 low cards needed the one repair (19 then delivered, 18 failed), so a delivered card usually costs two author calls. Only 3 low cards passed on the first answer (4.3, 5.1 and 5.4 s, each with a picture). Even a first-pass card with a picture took over 4 s: about 2.1 s for the author plus 2.2 s for the picture.

**Failures by reason (120 cards, 81 failed):** provider_timeout 58 (medium 19, high 39), explanation_over_budget 8 (prose 710-903 characters after the repair), explanation_bad_interactions 6 (a term anchored in a diagram or absent from the prose), explanation_layout 4 (a visual without prose on both sides), explanation_bad_diagram 4 (flow rules: one root, outcome without outgoing edges, process with one outgoing edge; once, invalid JSON), explanation_missing_visual 1. **No HTTP 429** from Fireworks or Recraft. The first answer was refused most often for over_budget (low: 20 of 37 repairs), then bad_diagram (10), bad_interactions (4) and layout (3).

**Pictures:** 37 made of 37 written by the author, with no retries needed and no silent server-side removal. However, **a delivered card's pictures are not shown in the live session** (see Browser cards). In 6 of 39 delivered cards the author wrote another visual mix than the question asked: a diagram and a picture for "two pictures" (3), a diagram added to a one-picture card (2), and one diagram for "two diagrams" (1). Server note: `deriveVisualRequirements` matches only the singular "diagram", so "two diagrams" sets no required diagram (explanation-visuals.js `DIAGRAM_WORD`).

**Text:** every delivered card had 3 suggested questions. 24 of 39 had at least one term pill. Prose median about 580 characters, none over 700. 13 were over 600 (accepted inside the 100-character tolerance).

**Browser render cost is small** when the card arrives. On reload, the whole card (text, SVG, picture) was visible 1.2-1.7 s after navigation, 7-445 ms after the card mounted. Live, the text and SVG appeared 0.1 s after the response. **Defect found: a picture is never shown live.** On insert, `IllustrationFigure` → `loadSource` reads `/api/learn/files/<id>?app=&board=`. That read is authorized through the board's file references (`file-access.js` `references`), which the just-inserted card has not saved yet, so it answers 404. The figure turns `failed` and does not retry. In all 3 live cards with pictures the request returned 404 at once, The same file returned 200 when the page was reloaded about 5 s later, and every picture then appeared. This needs a fix (retry `loadSource` after the board save, or read the owner's own source without the board scope) before pictures can count toward "time to visible".

### Caveats

- Local stack: the server ran on the owner's machine with local D1/R2; provider calls were real. Network latency from this machine to Fireworks and Recraft is included. Worker placement in production may differ.
- `SMALL_ENV=dev` (not `test`), so spend was enforced and recorded as in production. This also turns on dev-only behaviour elsewhere. Nothing in the /explain path depends on it.
- Fallback was OFF, so every timeout is a failure. With the chain on, a Fireworks timeout would have gone to Cerebras, then Gemini, adding time.
- High and most of medium were cut by the 8 s per-call deadline in `explanation-fast-chain.js` (`CHAIN_ENDPOINTS.fireworks.deadlineMs`). These numbers show those efforts do not fit that deadline. They do not show how long the efforts take when left to finish.
- 2 runs per prompt, 8 cards per effort and case cell: small samples. Treat per-case differences as indicative.
- Factual quality is not scored here. The blind review of the 39 delivered cards is done separately by the main session.
