# PLT-2(A) band clipped to the area under the curve (ml-plot-layouts.js, same commit as BAND-1)

Rule: a coordinate-plot with exactly ONE line series (not closed) and an x-band: the band is clipped to the area under that series (clipPath = the series' own drawn path, smooth or straight, closed down to the baseline: y = 0, or the bottom of the plot when 0 is outside the y range). Several series keep the full-height band. A band that is not an x-band (y-band) keeps its rectangle.

Files at 408 and 720: bell (bands [-1,1] and [-0.5,0.5], reference lines at 0, +-1, point callouts Mean and Standard deviation; the band colours are annotation indexes 2 (teal) and 3 (blue), so the wide band is the 3rd annotation, the narrow the 4th), ml-zscore-distribution-tails (the anomaly-density reference; its bands are the tails z < -3 and z > 3, where the curve is 0.004 high: the area under it is a sliver of about 1.5 px, so the clipped tails are nearly invisible: that is what the rule gives; the badges are gone (PLT-2(B)) and the labels are callouts), acf-pacf-stems (a stems plot has a scatter/stem series, not a line: its band keeps full height).
