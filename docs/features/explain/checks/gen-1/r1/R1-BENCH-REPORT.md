## Why G5 could not move it (found while reading the results)
The harness's 'planned kind missing' compares the delivered kind with the PLANNER's kind. In the records 84 of the 84 AFTER cards of set A that missed it (and 141 of the 142 AFTER cards over all sets) had a selected topical reference, and the card's shape id is the reference's kind, not the planner's: release 118's  replaces the plan's kind by the kind of an on-topic reference (learn-artifact.js; GEN-1 G3 is the spec's change of exactly this). So the author is asked for the reference's kind and writes it: against the EFFECTIVE plan kind (the shape the request carried) the delivery rate is 84 of 87 AFTER vs 80 of 84 BEFORE in set A, 96 of 98 vs 97 of 100 in set B, 28 of 29 vs 28 of 29 in set D; of all 'missing' cards with a reference, 267 of 279 (96 %) delivered the effective plan kind. PLAN-K (27 % 'planned kind missing') is therefore mostly the on-topic reference overriding the planner by design, not the model ignoring the plan; it is the R3 target (G3 + G4), not R1's. Not measured here: a card whose reference-replaced kind should differ from the planner's kind in a way a learner would notice.

## Decisions for the Reviewer
1. R1 shows no effect on planned kind delivery and a modest, unproven efficiency gain; it carries no regression (154-rule flags 0 vs 0, honest failures 4 vs 3). Release it with START-3 as planned (release B), or hold it until R3 shows its worth?
2. Redefine the harness metric for R2 and R3 as 'effective plan kind delivered' next to 'planner kind delivered', so the G3 effect (the planner's kind drawn instead of the reference's) is measured.
