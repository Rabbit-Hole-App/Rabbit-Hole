# Why did the scripts have wrong numbers, symbols, and reused examples?

Root-cause investigation. Evidence: prompt.txt, cards.json, scripts.json and scripts-gemini.json (the control, called V0), plus a controlled experiment on the 9 cards behind the slips (cards 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12), one call per card per variant, no retries, models Fireworks gpt-oss-120b and Gemini gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Files: variants.json (V1-V4), variants-v5.json, variants-v6.json, verify-variants.mjs, manual-verdicts.json, variant-summary.json, verify-out.json, verify-dump.txt.

## 0. Read this first: how far the evidence goes
- One sample per card per variant per model (90 calls). A change of 1 or 2 error items between variants is inside the noise of single samples; I did not repeat the control, so I cannot give a baseline rate for any single slip. The large moves (reuse 9 of 9 to 2 of 9, formula openers 8 of 9 to 0 of 54, symbol leaks) are the ones I trust.
- Numeric claims were recomputed by hand from the normalized numeric sentences that verify-variants.mjs prints (the script itself automatically checks symbols, openers, card-example reuse by regex, shared 5-word phrases with the card, softmax exponentials, and bootstrap percentages). Free-text factual claims were checked only for the flagged patterns (flat ground, "per second", runoff/formula), not exhaustively.
- Two extra variants beyond the six you asked for were run to test the recommended wording: V5 (the proposed v1.1) on both models and V6 (a stronger length wording) on Gemini only, 27 calls. Total trial spend is in section 7.

## 2. Phase A: what the existing data shows
Usage fields (Fireworks control): reasoning tokens / completion tokens per card: card 1 37/254, 4 46/258, 5 14/407, 6 48/456, 7 35/454, 9 23/606, 10 46/674, 11 39/527, 12 20/622. The lowest reasoning (14, 20, 23) sits on the cards with the worst arithmetic (5 bootstrap, 12 gradient descent, 9 softmax). finish_reason was "stop" on all 90 variant outputs and all 24 original outputs (no truncation). Gemini's usage reports completion tokens only (162 to 303 per script), no reasoning breakdown, so Gemini reasoning cannot be inspected.

LaTeX in the input: cards with $ spans in the body / labels with math: card 5: 12 / 1; card 6: 1 / 0; card 9: 3 / 5 (e^2.0, e^1.0, e^0.1, z, p); card 10: 1 / 0; card 11: 1 / 0; card 12: 7 / 0; cards 1, 4, 7: 0 / 0. Fireworks control leaked symbols on cards 9 (square brackets) and 12 (39 symbols); the prompt says "No LaTeX and no symbols" and the model obeyed it everywhere except where it was computing.

The prompt wording that pushes each behaviour (prompt.txt): "Work out any numbers in plain words" (invites computing); "(a) the one idea ... learners most often get wrong or find hardest" (the opener); "add what it does not say" is the only protection against reuse, and the card's visuals (labels such as "Insert 7 into the binary search tree / 7 < 8, 7 > 3, 7 > 6" and "e^2.0, e^1.0, e^0.1") are passed in the same message as the card, so the example is both in the input and nameable; the length is one soft range with the word "about".

## 5. What no prompt change fixes
1. Errors inherited from the card: the box-plot card (wrong fences). Fix the card; add a numeric check at card time. No script prompt can reconcile a script with a card that is wrong.
2. Arithmetic on numbers the model chose: Fireworks still had 1 to 2 errors per 9 outputs in V3, V4, V5 (a wrong sign, a step off by one, "6 units past"), including at medium reasoning. Gemini had none in 54 outputs but computes little. If a worked example with new numbers is wanted, a verifier pass (a second call or a recompute of every number) is still needed; if only the card's numbers are allowed (V1), a verifier is needed only for card errors, but reuse rises.
3. Unverifiable facts the model adds ("dozens of times every single second", a misconception that is wrong or garbled). A verifier or a "state no figure you cannot verify" rule; neither tested here.
4. Gemini undershooting length: no wording tested (seconds ceiling, word floor, content checklist) brought Gemini to the target; the usable fix is to set per-model targets (Gemini about 1.3 to 1.5 times the Fireworks word numbers) or to accept shorter Gemini audio.
5. Symbol leaks on Fireworks are not removed by wording alone (V3 leaked 3 of 9); a regex check on the returned text (no model call) removes them deterministically. Section D of prompt-v1.1.txt lists the characters.

So a verifier pass would still be needed later for the Fireworks path and for any prompt that asks for fresh numeric examples; for Gemini on V5 or V6 I found nothing a verifier would catch in these 18 outputs, but 18 single samples do not prove that.

## 6. Recommended prompt v1.1 (full text: prompt-v1.1.txt)
Changes against the current prompt, each tested above:
1. Replace "Work out any numbers in plain words so the learner can follow them by ear" with: "Use only numbers that appear in the card, or trivially simple whole numbers you can verify. Do not compute anything harder than adding or doubling. If a number is not in the card, describe it in words without a value." (also remove "worked" from the Balanced and Detailed depth lines.)
2. Add to the "do not restate" paragraph: "Treat everything in the card text and in the labels of its visuals as already said and shown: do not reuse its example, its numbers, its labelled items or its steps, and do not restate its definitions or formulas. Pick a different example, or none."
3. Add an OPENING section: "Vary your opening. Never start with "The hardest part" or "The part that trips" or any announcement of what is hard. Open on a concrete image, on a question the learner might be asking, or on the common misconception itself."
4. Replace the depth bullets with seconds and words: Brief "aim for 60 to 80 seconds of speech, about 180 to 240 words"; Balanced "about 90 seconds, about 240 to 300 words"; Detailed "100 to 130 seconds, about 300 to 390 words"; then "Stay inside the range for your depth: do not go over the top of it, and do not stop under the bottom of it unless the idea is complete. Never pad."
5. Gemini only: use the V6 depth block (word floors and a content checklist per depth; section C of the file).
6. Outside the prompt: a regex check for the symbol characters on the returned script.
Known wart: rule 1 and rule 2 conflict on card numbers; the models resolved it by choosing simple whole numbers (V5: Fireworks 2 minor slips, Gemini 0). A cleaner wording is given in the file header, untested.
Result of the proposed v1.1 on the 9 trial cards: Fireworks (V5) 2 minor numeric slips, 0 symbol leaks, 2 reuse, 0 formula openers, 8 of 9 lengths inside the window; Gemini (V5 / V6) 0 numeric slips, 0 symbol leaks, 2 / 0 reuse, 0 formula openers, 1 / 2 of 9 inside the window (rest under).
