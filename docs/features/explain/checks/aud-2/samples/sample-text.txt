The subtle point that trips most learners is that precision looks at the subset of cases you labeled positive, while recall looks at the whole set of actual positives. In other words, precision asks, “Of the items I claimed are yes, how many truly are yes?” Recall asks, “Of all the items that really are yes, how many did I manage to label as yes?” Imagine a spam filter that flags 100 emails as spam.
