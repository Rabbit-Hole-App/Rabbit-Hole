## Composition contract and authoring stop

- **Ordered stages or an operation between panels:** `panels`, with 2–3 children, optional `label` up to 40 characters and at most two neighbouring links whose `operation` is at most 24 characters. Children are leaf kinds, `block-stack` or `token-links`; no nested composition.
- **Plain side-by-side with no operation:** `comparison` with `variant: panels`, using `heading` up to 40 characters on each child. Do not substitute text columns.
- **Stacked quantities:** `bar-chart` with `variant: stacked`, a unit, 2–8 strictly positive parts and optional total; parts sum to total within rounding.
- Neither composition form is in the author's tool schema yet. References may use both; this queue does not modify the tool schema or prompts.
- The owner confirmed **`1944b48cf24c9d6bd7f53b34ddbe258d1c6113df`** as the schema pin for batch 1 after approval. One data-only number-formatting commit will follow without changing the schema. Rebase onto main when **release 133** lands.
- `shared: classes/colours` works only when every panel is a bar chart. `axis` requires coordinate plots with matching labels, ranges, units and scales.
