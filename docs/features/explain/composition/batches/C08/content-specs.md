## C08 architecture-flow QA revision

Actual native block-stack merge inputs are rendered AFTER their owning block. Therefore all three token-position architecture details use three blocks: (1) categorical selected token ID and position, whose two downstream independent model-level E[id] and P[p] lookup inputs merge by addition; (2) the separate same-case combined-input tensor; (3) the repeated decoder block. The selected indices feed lookups, and the lookup results feed the sum before the combined vector reaches the decoder. Overview's position lookup is the fixed function; Standard uses the supplied learned table; Deep uses its edited learned row and actual probe sum. Both lookup tables/functions and addition remain outside decoder repeats. No already-combined tensor may precede the independent lookup fork. All main grids, values, source associations and teaching points remain unchanged.
