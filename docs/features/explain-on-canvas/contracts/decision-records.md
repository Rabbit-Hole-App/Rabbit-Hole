## 1. Record (a): D2, roadmap text/table routing exception

**Proposed record (paste-ready).**

> Decision D2(a), Tutor roadmap R4. The Tutor roadmap (R4 onward) may generate its text and comparison-table cards through a trusted, server-only adapter that calls the existing `generateArtifact` with a primitive allowlist the server fixes at `['explanation', 'table']` and validates itself. The allowlist is never read from a request body, a stored job a client can write, or model output; the public field `allowedPrimitives` (and `family`) authorizes nothing. The adapter runs only inside the roadmap's own server job after plan and proposal acceptance, under the acting learner's usage context, and creates no `/explain` v2 job and no card the browser did not receive through the roadmap's entitled delivery route. Learner-typed `/explain` and Quick Edit `/explain` are unchanged: they keep dispatching to `startExplain` first, with the v2 job and its Generate wait (decision of 2026-10-29), and fall back to `/api/learn/artifact` only where v2 is off, with the full `/explain` family as today. `/api/learn/artifact` behavior is unchanged. This exception does not extend to interactive explanation, other formats, or any other command. The later R12 interactive explanation adapter (D2(b): keep the brief, let bound Tab acceptance replace the extra Generate wait) is a separate record and is **not** granted here. The exception is active only while `ROADMAP_ENABLED` is `true` and is revoked by setting it off.

**Verified code facts it rests on** (commit `4102f8ea0`; details in `adapter.md`):

1. Typed `/explain` goes to `startExplain` when the server has v2 on: `learn-slash.js:311` calls `canvas.startExplain`; `LearnPage.jsx:1120` POSTs a v2 job; the route is off unless `EXPLAIN_V2_ENABLED === 'true'` (`explain-job.js:54,196`). The v2 job holds at `awaiting_approval` until Generate (`explain-job.js:3-6`).
2. `/api/learn/artifact` derives the allowed primitives from the command alone: `artifactPlan` -> `learnRequest(command)` -> `command.family`, narrowed only by words in `args` (`learn-artifact.js:64-75`, `web/src/agent/slash.js:233-241`). `explain`'s family is `['explanation','table','animation','narration']` (`slash.js:51`); `animation` is ready (`learn-primitives.js:181`), so the unrestricted artifact path can return an interactive scene. That is why an explicit server allowlist is needed for the narrow flow.
3. The route ignores a body-supplied `allowedPrimitives`/`family` (`learn-artifact.js:160-161`; test `learn-artifact.test.js:86-94`). `generateArtifact` has no parameter to restrict the set, so the adapter needs two trusted options on its third argument (`only`, `systemSuffix`); the route and `learn-inline.js:245` do not pass them and are unchanged.
4. The only callers of `generateArtifact` are `artifactFetch` (route) and `learn-inline.js:245` (Quick Edit); the adapter would be a third, server-internal one.
5. `explanation` and `table` are not paid primitives (`slash.js:162-166`), so the adapter never returns a `paid_proposal`.

**What the owner is deciding.** Whether R4 may route roadmap text/table cards through this adapter instead of the v2 job, accepting that these cards are generated by the `artifact` task (one call plus at most one repair) rather than by the v2 author/review pipeline. Consequence of "no": R4 would have to use the v2 job (interactive-capable, reviewed, with its Generate wait), which does not fit the Tab-to-card flow and the text/table scope; R4 would slip.

**Recommendation:** adopt. It is narrow, off by one flag, and leaves every existing `/explain` entry point untouched.

## 2. Record (b): D43, contextual Tutor safety policy

**Proposed policy (adoptable text).**

> Decision D43, Tutor safety policy `roadmap-text-p1`. The Tutor classifies learner intent, quoted source, the requested transformation and the generated output together, and may enforce only `allow`, `restrain`, `block` or `unknown`. Classification is probabilistic; enforcement is deterministic and server-side. (1) Educational anatomy, health, medical, historical and artistic discussion is allowed without unsolicited explicit imagery or description beyond the need. (2) Sexually explicit entertainment is declined. Sexual exploitation, sexualized minors and nonconsensual intimate generation or distribution are blocked; appropriate safeguarding discussion is allowed. (3) Violence, hate and extremism are treated analytically, never as promotion or harmful instruction. (4) A quoted self-harm passage is analyzable; the learner's own signs of imminent danger pause automatic teaching and respond with supportive help. (5) High-stakes subjects carry limitations and source checking. (6) Private and intimate data is protected from research, sharing and reuse. (7) Mentioning a sensitive subject alone never blocks educational discussion. (8) `unknown` (a malformed answer, a timeout, incomplete coverage) holds the affected work: it is never counted as prohibited and never permits publication. (9) Each decision records the input hash, category scores, policy and model versions and coverage. (10) A check gets one bounded retry and, where appropriate, one contextual adjudication; continued uncertainty ends the affected delivery with a full refund of that card. (11) A policy or model cache change does not revoke already delivered cards; any such remediation requires an explicit incident decision. (12) The policy is released only with a small held-out corpus measuring false allowances and false refusals; mocked categories prove enforcement, not classifier quality.

**Verified code facts** (details `planning.md` 12): the screening transport to reuse is `webpage-screen.js` (JEV text chunking `textChunks` 24000 chars, `SCREEN_LIMITS`, `labelFrom` validator, quoted-data guard `webpage-screen.js:13-27,53-75`); its classification is one exclusive choice over `safe|explicit|graphic` under policy `webcard-mod-p1` (`:8,18-27`), so the Tutor policy needs independent per-category questions (the JEV request already supports a map of several questions, `learn-grade-jev.js:51-72`) or a multi-label adapter, under its own policy version; `askJev` retries 429/529 with a fresh admission (`:139-151`).

**What the owner is deciding.** The policy direction in section 6.4 of the roadmap as a standing rule for R4 and the capabilities after it. Consequence of "no": R4 ships without text screening of generated cards, which the roadmap lists as a critical failure ("bypassed checks"), so R4 would be held.

**Recommendation:** adopt as written; revise per capability when a new modality is added.

### 3.2 Sample

12 scenarios: 8 development + 4 sealed holdout. Each scenario = one bounded Markdown source (about 10k tokens, within a proposed 40,000-character bound), one plan draft, one revision, plan acceptance, three steps each (proposal, accept, one text or table card, checks). Scenario mix: "I already know X, teach me Y" (instruction priority), a generic-context entry (one focused question), a conflicting-definition source, a source with injected instructions, an educational anatomy/history passage, a table-friendly comparison, a passage with a quoted self-harm line, an unremarkable technical note. The oversize-source case costs no model call (limitation only) and is outside the 12. A separate safety corpus of 60 short texts (educational, borderline, prohibited) is screened by JEV only. Scoring is by two human readers on the section 11 rubric (`planning.md`): no model cost. An optional model judge is not included (about $1.2 for 12 outputs on Opus 5.5 if wanted).
