Blue · shared actionsPrimary fill and selected ink #375D92. Filled hover #2B4B78, pressed #203858, white glyph. Selected surface #EAF1FB, border #ADC4E5, hover #DDE9F8, pressed #D0E0F4. Focus outline #426DB2, 2 px, +2 px offset.
Claude red · Interactive#934723 ink and #F8ECE3 surface for Interactive identity and scene data accents. Shared controls use blue. The mode pills are text-only.
Semantic colorGreen #267657 for completion and positive status. Destructive and error roles use #9D3D39 with #F9EBEA where a tint is needed.
Source headerSame title role as Explanation and Interactive. Inter → Liberation Sans → Arial → sans-serif, 26.4 px / 700 / 1.17, tracking −0.023 em, ink #202723. At card content width ≤370 px, use 21.6 px title and 15 px horizontal inset. Standard inset 24 px, top 17 px, bottom 11 px. Type pill is 26 px minimum, 13 px / 700, radius 999 px and padding 1 × 11 px. Use the rename when set, otherwise the filename. When a rename differs from the filename, the optional filename line is 14 px / 650 / 1.35 in #5D675F, 4 px below the title. Put technical file details in file info.
Markdown and PDF pillsText only, 26 px minimum height, 999 px radius, 1 × 11 px padding, 13 px / 700 / 1.45. Opaque #EAF1FB fill, #375D92 text and 1 px #ADC4E5 border.
Selected text actionsUse the existing Highlighter, Lightbulb and MessageSquarePlus glyphs at 15 px, stroke 2. Targets 35 × 35 px, 8 px radius, gap 3 px. Popover surface #FFFEFA with 1 px #DEDFD8 border, 10 px radius and 4 px inset. Selected text uses #D0E0F4 with #202723 ink.
Composer shellUses the teaching card shell, including the 26.4 px title, 24 px inset, 18 px card radius, one scrolling body and content height cap. The footer keeps tools and the resize grip visible while the body scrolls. The outlined grip is 26 × 26 px with a 7 px radius and the same 14 px glyph and resize behavior as the cards.
Prompt and toolsInput text 15 px / 400 / 1.45, control labels 13 px / 650 / 1.45. Standard 35 px controls, 8 px radius, 1 px outline. Send and Stop are the same rectangular blue primary action. The message grows with wrapped text and line breaks. Longer drafts scroll within the capped card body.
