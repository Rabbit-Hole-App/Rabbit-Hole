Control templates

Each template works on its own. A card typically needs zero to three primary controls.

Playback and compact card actions use soft blue at rest, with a #EAF1FB surface, #ADC4E5 border and #375D92 ink. Hover uses #DDE9F8, pressed uses #D0E0F4, and keyboard focus uses a 2 px #426DB2 outline with a 2 px offset. Timeline scrubbers use #426DB2. Shared switches use #375D92 on, #2B4B78 on hover and #203858 on press. Main app actions use solid blue #375D92 with white ink. Sliders and choices that change a scene’s experiment use the warm accent #B86232, selected surface #F8ECE3, and ink #934723. Explanation and Interactive pills are text labels.

UI inventory

Use the first three rows to build the 12 references. The remaining rows cover other lesson types and existing card actions.

Shared card shell
Explanation or Interactive pill, title, teaching copy, optional figure and caption, optional source and Focus actions, spacing, responsive sizing, and loading or recovery states.
Teaching visuals
Class dots and legend, split comparison rows, formula rows, numeric table with a featured row, metric tiles, callout bands, paired bar rows, active decision paths, plots, vote tiles, and highlighted data grids.
Inputs for the 12
Labeled slider and value, compact exclusive selector, and a single action button. Show the result through a Why line, active path, current score, tally, or other state-linked readout.
Timed and richer scenes
Play, pause, replay, scrubber and current time. Reset is a secondary action. Ordered index, multi-select, switch, and selectable or draggable targets depend on the renderer.
Controls to register
Dropdown for many or long choices, precise number input or stepper, and collapsed secondary settings with a changed-state cue. The examples below the card shells show their appearance and behavior.
Explanation actions
Source passage, term definitions, suggested questions, same-card or linked-card follow-up, and selection highlights or notes. Use the app’s existing actions and persist their card and source context.
