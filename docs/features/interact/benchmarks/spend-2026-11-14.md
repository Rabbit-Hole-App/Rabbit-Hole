## Findings

1. **The ledger understates the spend by 4.91 USD** (jobs at `awaiting_approval` and one orphaned job). That is by design for jobs that never finish; the table above is the full count.
2. **Rows of the fast path after the no-approval change carry no design cost in their own diagnostics for most runs**: in the final set only 2 of 8 fast rows show `designCostUsd`; the others read 0 because the job's `calls` list no longer holds the `design_fill` call. The true fill cost is about 0.003 per row (the fast record's `costUsd` is 0.0024 to 0.0033), so the undercount is about 0.02 USD in the window. Not fixed here (out of scope of the reconciliation).
3. **A merge dropped 38 ledger rows and the evidence folders** (heights, opus, pick, 19 judge-findings files): the coverage commit `091c6e776` carried a tree without them, and the merge into `design/fast-path` kept its version. They are restored on this branch (`ledger.jsonl` now has 90 rows: the 88 of `363f9b49a` plus the two fast-run rows lost earlier, `ej_f1b649a2` and `ej_1ae77c05`, rebuilt from the clone's diagnostics).
