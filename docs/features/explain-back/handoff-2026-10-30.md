## Try it

Preview: https://rabbit-hole-web-dev-explain-back.tryrabbithole.workers.dev (build `408f4cc5f`; the candidate adds only a
check fix and docs). Sign in, open or create a canvas, then insert a lesson block, Explain back.

1. Type an answer with a deliberate mistake, then Submit answer. Within about 15 s you see Tutor feedback, its three
   sections and a blue N/10 badge.
2. Revise answer, fix the mistake and submit. Attempt 2 is reviewed against attempt 1: the feedback names what you fixed
   and the badge changes. Switch between attempts with the selector; attempt 1 stays as it was.
3. Open sketch: one panel opens on the right with the question still visible. Draw, Pin, Close and reopen: the marks and
   the content dot stay. Submit a sketch-only answer.
4. Record voice with the text empty and Sketch closed: Stop, play it back, submit. The typed field never changes. The
   transcript is made only when you submit (this is the first real transcription; it has not run yet).
5. Drag from a note's or a Group's top or bottom port to the green Input at the card's upper left: one pill appears. Its ×
   disconnects it.
