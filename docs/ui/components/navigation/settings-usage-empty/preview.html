Learning analytics

To improve Rabbit Hole and find problems, Rabbit Hole records analytics events about how learning activity goes. They use internal account and canvas identifiers and contain: whether an assessed answer was met, partly met or not met and whether help was involved, how it was assessed (a rubric match, or an AI evaluator and its version), the AI model and prompt version, timings, error types and AI credit usage.

Browser error reports hold the kind of error and the page area, never the error message or page content. The support reference you receive after sending feedback links your report to those technical records.

These analytics events do not include the text of your messages, questions or answers, your goal or suggestion wording, your files or what you draw. That statement is about analytics only: your canvases, chats and submitted answers are still stored in your account so Rabbit Hole works.

Only the Rabbit Hole owner can view analytics, with one exception: if you accept public-board measurement, a creator sees aggregate reports for their own public learning boards only, never who you are or what you did individually. People who made a canvas you open never see your individual records.
