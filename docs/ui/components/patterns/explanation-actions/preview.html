Why do leaves change color in fall?

Leaves look green because they contain . As days get shorter in fall, the green fades and other colors become easier to see.

Yellow and orange come from that were already in the leaf. Some trees also make pigments in fall that turn their leaves red or purple.

Different trees have different mixes of pigments, so two trees on the same street can turn different colors.
