2. Do NOT press Generate on any Motion or image card, and do not confirm /video. Nothing here needs paid generation.
3. Stop at the first FAIL of an item, write what you saw, take one screenshot, go to the next item. A FAIL does not end the sitting.
4. Write PASS only for what you saw. If you could not reach the step (no notebook card, no second login) write SKIP and why. A blank item is NOT accepted.

Write PASS/FAIL/SKIP in the last column, or fill a copy of owner-results.json and run: node owner-receipt.mjs receipt owner-results.json receipt.md
