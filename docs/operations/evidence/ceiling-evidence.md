## 3. Does US$3.50 accommodate the bounded workflow?
Measured per-call actuals (FACT, motion-runs M5/M6 ledger.json; handback-full.md): Director 0.061-0.068; storyboard 0.147-0.252; Author 0.50 (10 s), 0.98 (30 s), 0.37 (9:16 15 s); reviews 0.05-0.09 each; patch repair 0.086 (10 s), 0.131 (30 s). Completed model-built jobs settled 0.71 (M4 15 s), 0.93 (M1 15 s), 1.12 (M5 10 s, one repair), 1.20 (M2-r2 30 s), 1.76 (M6 30 s, one repair).

The ceiling binds on the PEAK of committed money (actuals so far + in-flight bounds), not on the final cost. Peak = C + both first-pass review bounds; with a repair: C + first-pass review actuals + repair actual + both second-pass review bounds.

| Scenario | C (Director+storyboard+Author actual) | Peak, no repair | Peak, one repair | vs 3.50 | vs 2.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 s, measured M1/M4 type | 0.82 (M1: 0.919 total) | 0.82 + 1.46 = 2.28 | 0.82 + 0.15 + 0.10 + 1.46 = 2.53 | fits, margin 1.0 | no repair path fails |
| 30 s, measured M6 | 0.068+0.252+0.978 = 1.298 | 1.298 + 1.461 = 2.76 | 1.298 + 0.159 + 0.131 + 1.461 = 3.05 | fits, margin 0.45 | fails |
| Failed job's own numbers (C 1.278, assume 16 frames: reviews 1.505) | 1.278157 | 1.278 + 1.505 = 2.78 | 1.278 + ~0.15 + ~0.13 + 1.505 = ~3.06 | fits, margin ~0.44 | fails (it did) |
| Worst plausible: Author at its 64000-token cap | 0.07+0.25+1.40 = 1.72 | 3.18 | about 3.9 | no-repair fits; repair does NOT | fails |

Agrees with failure-handling-lane-state.md: guard peak 2.74-2.80 without repair, 3.05-3.13 with one; floor 2.56 (five required calls at output caps: 0.32 + 0.32 + 1.28 + 0.32 + 0.32; spend-bound.mjs MANDATORY_PATH, floorMicros). 3.00 clears a no-repair job but fails the 30 s repair case by 0.05 (3.05 > 3.00); 3.50 clears it by 0.45. The per-call checks also pass at 3.50: Author call need about 1.4 + committed 0.2-0.3 + least-ahead 0.64 = 2.3 < 3.5 (fails at 2.00 under the new reserve-ahead rule).

Does a ceiling raise alone fix THIS failure? Money part: yes (INFERENCE, the failed job's refused step needs about 0.75-0.8 against 3.50 - 1.278 = 2.22 left; both reviews 1.5 would fit). But a raise alone changes nothing until the orchestrator is redeployed (live orchestrator is 32d86ee7 with 2.00), and it does not retroactively fix job 61f70171 (settled and failed; refund is a separate owner command, refund-dry-run.txt: 127.8157 credits, 0 refunded). It is not a guarantee of success; limits that can still refuse or stop a job:
- 9 calls (`MOTION_MAX_CALLS`, fly.orchestrator.toml): a storyboard revision + Author repair path is exactly 9 calls (M5 used 9 and succeeded); a tenth is refused `call_limit` (orchestrator.mjs:365). Per-kind policy caps also apply (brief 1, storyboard 2, composition 2, review 4).
- 3300 s total (`MOTION_JOB_TIMEOUT_S`): the failed job used 635 s; completed dev jobs 441-1058 s (M6 1058 s). 60 s jobs are unavailable. Production renderer timing is unmeasured (INFERENCE: dev renderer numbers). On timeout the job ends `cause limit` (:412).
- Worker hold: `MOTION_JOB_RESERVATION_USD "8.96"` (wrangler.rabbit-hole-prod.jsonc:105) is the grant; effective ceiling = min(grant, ceiling) so 3.50 never exceeds it (:295). The Worker holds 8.96 and releases the unused part. The learner's/owner's allowance must be able to cover the 8.96 hold at admission (learn-video.js:419 `admitSpend`, `allowance_exhausted`); unrelated to the orchestrator ceiling.
- Account/video allowance across attempts: `admitMotion` computes `left = reservation - consumedMicros` (learn-video.js:409); a failed attempt's real cost counts toward it.
- Preflight floor 2.56 (new orchestrator, orchestrator.mjs:140-148): below it every job is refused before any spend; 3.50 passes (post-fix-lane-state.md: local preflight accepts 3.50, refuses 2.00). Policy-class refusals (`policy_rejected`, `unknown_cost_bound`, a call above the policy's tokens/bytes/images, a resend) are not helped by any ceiling.
- 45/60 s: the policy keeps them unavailable (handback-full.md section 7); a 15 s job is unaffected.
- Paused flag: `MOTION_GENERATION_PAUSED "1"` (wrangler.rabbit-hole-prod.jsonc:119): only the owner emails in MOTION_ACCEPTANCE_EMAILS can start; R2 unpauses.
- The renderer-class limits (420 s render, container length at 60 s) and content failures (blocking review finding after the one repair) are separate and still end a job.
- Real provider limits: the Anthropic workspace usage limit (the earlier 2026-10-21 / 10-25 "workspace API usage limits" 400) and the shared validation cap (US$20, about 1.75 left per the lane rules) are outside this ceiling.
